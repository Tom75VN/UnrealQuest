# Quest POI number texture attribution

`UIQuestPOINumberIcons.tga` is an imported copy of Blizzard's
`Interface/WorldMap/UI-QuestPoi-NumberIcons`, extracted with Forever FrameXML.

The original texture stores the circle states and the number glyphs 1-25 in
separate 32x32 atlas regions. Forever's `POIButtonTemplate` draws a 20x20
button with 32x32 normal, pushed and highlight textures, while
`QuestUtils.ApplyStandardTexturesToPOI` selects the normal, selected, pushed
and highlight circle regions. The older numbered POI convention combines a
yellow glyph with the dark circle and reverses the followed quest to a black
glyph on a yellow circle.

`tools/import_quest_poi_numbers.py` preserves the complete source atlas without
precomposing its layers and converts it to the client's proven RLE TGA form.
UnrealQuest places the circle, glyph and hover glow on separate one-texture
Buttons. The source PNG is not bundled.

`QuestPOINumbers.tga` is generated by `tools/make_quest_poi_markers.py`: the
plain glyph shapes of `UIQuestPOINumberIcons.tga` recoloured solid (`#2c1501`
in rows 0-3, `#f8c662` in rows 4-7) and upscaled to twice their definition
(512x512, 64px cells) for the quest POI badges.
